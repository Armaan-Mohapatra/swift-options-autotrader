## Gemini review: reject the drop-in live implementation

- Proposed interfaces TradingEngine, BrokerageAdapterProtocol, get_account_profile and verify_write_capabilities are not validated against this repo. Existing pieces are LiveDispatcher, PaperOrderEngine, RobinhoodMCPBroker, RobinhoodLiveBroker, MCPReadOnlyOptionData and the existing CLI app.py. Do not add a parallel live ownership/PNL ledger with float values.
- Preserve the reviewed one contract, LOTTO eligible, no averaging down, close all matching Swift-owned holdings on partial exits, 10% entry slippage, $750 net realized price P&L loss threshold (excludes fees in current implementation), $10,000 proposed aggregate ceiling subject to buying power; not Gemini's $655 gross/9999/999, or unapproved 45-second freshness override. A configuration threshold is not an absolute maximum potential loss.
- Existing adapter expects ref_id, filled_quantity, accepted_at and fills. Previously observed actual no-fill broker response contained state unconfirmed/confirmed/cancelled, processed_quantity, pending_quantity, canceled_quantity, created_at/updated_at and legs; no echoed ref_id or accepted_at. Actual positive-fill shape remains unverified. Do not fake fees/fills/acceptance times, match ambiguous orders by ticker, substitute requested limit for fill price or infer that cancellation acknowledgement means terminal cancellation.
- Gemini's proposed runtime can repeat submissions, fails to reserve pending-order exposure, does not validate future/NaN/negative inputs, does not preserve exact broker fill IDs or durable unknown-order evidence, can count cumulative fills twice, can block handling of older exits, uses wall clock for polling and gross loss accounting, and can create a second incompatible SQLite journal. A typed phrase cannot prove Robinhood permission. Provider-required approvals must remain effective.
- Actual Windows common.ps1 pins PAPER_ONLY true, LIVE_ENABLED false, ALLOW_BROKER_WRITES false, BROKER_BACKEND paper. The manifest/security guard, startup CLI and live service integration are separate from flags. Do not flip or bypass these settings. Existing app has paper-replay, paper-dispatch-service, mvp-startup-check and mvp-review, not the proposed verify-pipeline/canary-live CLI. Dashboard endpoint is /api/status, not /health. Python is under releases/<active SHA>/.venv/Scripts/python.exe. Use supported manage.ps1 for restart, not unverified Restart-ScheduledTask.
- Robinhood official Trading with your agent documentation: eligible orders may be executed without per-order approval when account settings permit; certain orders still require approval. https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-with-your-agent/ . No local setting substitutes for broker authorization. A $0.01 limit can fill, and submission/cancel-only evidence does not certify actual fills or recovery.
